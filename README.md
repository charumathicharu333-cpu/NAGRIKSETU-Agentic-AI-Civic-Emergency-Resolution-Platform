# NagrikSetu AI 🌍

### An Agentic Civic Operations & Intelligence Platform

NagrikSetu AI is an AI-powered civic operations platform designed to transform traditional complaint management into predictive, intelligent, and citizen-centric urban governance.

The platform helps citizens report civic issues while enabling authorities to identify emerging risks, prioritize incidents, coordinate response teams, prevent potential emergencies, and verify completed work through AI-assisted workflows.

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Civic Digital Twin:** Visualizes area-level incidents, infrastructure risks, affected populations, and response resources.
* **AI Risk Prediction:** Identifies potential civic risks and generates illustrative risk forecasts.
* **Response Simulation:** Compares possible response scenarios to support faster decision-making.
* **Resource Optimization:** Recommends suitable response teams based on distance, workload, skills, and equipment.
* **Citizen Crowd Intelligence:** Groups related reports to identify common civic incidents.
* **Duplicate Incident Detection:** Finds potentially matching reports and connects citizens to existing incidents.
* **Evidence Intelligence:** Assists in reviewing before-and-after images and location evidence.
* **Resolution Confidence:** Combines available verification signals to assess the evidence supporting a resolution.
* **Citizen Verification:** Enables citizens to confirm, challenge, or partially accept a reported resolution.
* **Preventive Mission Mode:** Supports proactive inspections and maintenance based on identified risks.
* **AI Emergency Briefs:** Generates concise incident summaries for officials and response teams.
* **Explainable AI Decisions:** Displays the factors behind incident prioritization and recommendations.
* **Agent Handoff Visualization:** Shows how triage, prioritization, resource allocation, and verification workflows interact.
* **Emergency War Room:** Provides a focused command-center interface for monitoring critical incidents and response progress.
* **Civic Impact Dashboard:** Tracks incidents, verified resolutions, response performance, and estimated impact.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Improve civic issue reporting and incident coordination.
* Identify risks before they escalate into major emergencies.
* Reduce duplicate reports and improve shared situational awareness.
* Support efficient allocation of public response resources.
* Increase transparency and accountability in resolution workflows.
* Build citizen trust through evidence-based verification and feedback.

## 💡 What Makes NagrikSetu AI Different?

Unlike a conventional complaint-management application, NagrikSetu AI brings together predictive risk analysis, citizen-generated intelligence, resource recommendations, coordinated response workflows, and citizen-led resolution verification in one platform.
